# Inix v0.1.15

Our browser is getting even better at helping you stay private online.

## What's new
### New Feature: Better Shortcut Management

In our latest update, we've improved the way shortcuts are created and managed in Inix. This means that you'll now see a more streamlined and intuitive shortcut creation process, making it easier to get started with your favorite features.

## Improvements
### Settings Page Redesign

We've made some exciting changes to our settings page, including a refreshed layout and improved navigation. These changes should make it even easier for you to find and customize the features that matter most to you.

## Fixes
### NSIS Create Desktop Shortcut Issue

A pesky issue with creating desktop shortcuts has been fixed, ensuring that Inix installs and launches properly on your computer. Your settings just got a bit more reliable.
