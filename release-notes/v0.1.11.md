# Inix v0.1.11

We're excited to bring you a new version of Inix, our fast private desktop browser. This update includes significant improvements for users.

## What's New
### Uninstall Process Improvements
When uninstalling Inix, we've enhanced the process to ensure a smoother experience. The new feature provides more detailed information during the uninstallation process and retry logic to help you complete the removal of Inix.

## Improvements
### Performance and Stability
The update includes several performance enhancements that improve overall stability and responsiveness. These changes are designed to provide a better user experience, especially for users with slower internet connections or older systems.

### Bug Fixes
We've addressed several issues reported by our community, including fixes for crashes, freezes, and other usability problems.

## Fixes
- Fixed an issue where the Inix UI would sometimes become unresponsive after a long period of inactivity.
- Resolved an issue that caused incorrect data to be displayed in some settings options.
- Improved the performance of the browser's search functionality.

## Notes
This update can be downloaded immediately and installed via our official installer. If you encounter any issues or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team at [support@example.com](mailto:support@example.com).
