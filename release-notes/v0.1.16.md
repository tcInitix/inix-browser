# Inix v0.1.16

We've made some exciting updates to improve your Inix experience!

## Improvements

### Settings Page Refresh

Our settings page has received a refresh to make it easier for you to customize and personalize your browsing experience.

### Bug Fixes

A pesky issue with creating desktop shortcuts has been fixed, ensuring that Inix installs and launches properly on your computer. Your settings just got a bit more reliable.

## Fixes

- **Updated Installer Behavior**: We've updated the installer behavior to quit the app before launching the NSIS installer, reducing potential issues with shortcut creation.
- **Improved Release Notes Generation**: Our release notes generation process has been improved to provide more accurate and concise updates for users like you.

That's it for this update! Enjoy the new features and improvements in Inix v0.1.16.
