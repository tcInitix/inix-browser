# Inix v0.1.30

**Faster and More Reliable Upgrades**

## What's new

### Installer Improvements
The installer now handles cases where previous versions' uninstallers may crash, ensuring a smoother upgrade process.

## Improvements

* Improved bookmark bar behavior: When you click outside of the bar, it will now close correctly, even if you were in a popup or scrolling through tabs.
* Enhanced installation cleaning: Inix will now remove all necessary registry entries and staging folders after an installation, making it easier to start fresh.

## Fixes

* Various minor bugs and issues that may have caused crashes or unexpected behavior during upgrades.
